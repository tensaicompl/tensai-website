The Five Failure Modes Diagnostic
1
Data Foundation
58--85% cite data quality as the primary barrier
Ask yourself: Can your data team deliver a clean, labelled dataset for a new use case within two weeks?
High
2
Organisational and Leadership
77--95% of failures are organisational, not technical
Ask yourself: Does your CEO review AI portfolio performance quarterly with the same rigour as financial results?
Critical
3
Missing MLOps Infrastructure
Production requires 5--10x pilot investment
Ask yourself: Can you deploy a model update to production without manual intervention?
High
4
Last Mile Problem
7 frictions between capability and organisation
Ask yourself: Have the end users of your AI systems been involved in design decisions?
Medium-High
5
Cost Escalation
2.5--4x cost multiplier from pilot to production
Ask yourself: Does your AI business case include change management and ongoing maintenance costs?
High
Scoring
Score your organisation 1--5 against each mode. Totals below 15 indicate structural readiness gaps that will prevent scaling regardless of technical capability. The organisational and leadership mode (row 2) is the single strongest predictor of failure.
The Five Failure Modes diagnostic. Each mode compounds the others. CTOs should score their organisation against each dimension. Sources: MIT 2025, Gartner 2026, Stanford 2026, RAND 2025.
