0--12 months
Phase 1
Centralised Delivery
10--15 person team
  • All AI development in-house
  • Build foundational data pipelines
  • Establish governance baseline
  • Deliver first 3--5 production models
  • Create reusable templates and tooling

Phase 2
Hub-and-Spoke
Central + 3--4 BU spokes
  • Central team shifts to platform and standards
  • BU spokes own domain-specific models
  • Shared infrastructure, distributed execution
  • Cross-BU knowledge sharing forums
  • Governance becomes embedded, not bolted on

Advisory Model
Small central governance + empowered BUs

  • BUs fully own AI development lifecycle
  • Platform team maintains shared infrastructure
  • Standards enforced through automation, not review
