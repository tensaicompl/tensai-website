# Diagram Brief — Operating Model & Maturity

## Title
The Four Stages and the Wall

## Single idea the diagram conveys
AI maturity progresses through four stages — Experimentation, Standardisation, Scaling, AI Factory — but the transition from Stage 2 to Stage 3 is a structural wall, not a gradual slope, and it is where most organisations stall.

## Layout description
A horizontal composition with four stage blocks arranged left to right, connected by arrows — but the arrow between Stage 2 and Stage 3 is visually disrupted by a wall element.

### The four stages

Four equal-width rectangular blocks arranged in a single horizontal row. Each block contains:

1. **Stage 1 — Experimentation** — Inside the block: three small disconnected dots scattered irregularly (isolated pilots, no connections). Label beneath: "Experimentation".
2. **Stage 2 — Standardisation** — Inside the block: three dots now connected by thin lines to a single central dot above them (the CoE hub, teams connected to it). Label beneath: "Standardisation".
3. **Stage 3 — Scaling** — Inside the block: a small horizontal bar at the top (the platform), with five dots hanging below it, each connected to the bar but not to each other (teams building independently on shared infrastructure). Label beneath: "Scaling".
4. **Stage 4 — AI Factory** — Inside the block: the same platform bar at top, five dots below connected to it, but now a circular feedback arrow loops from the bottom of the dots back up to the platform bar (continuous improvement loop). Label beneath: "AI Factory".

### The connections

- A thin horizontal arrow connects Stage 1 to Stage 2. Smooth, unbroken.
- Between Stage 2 and Stage 3: a vertical element — a thick, short vertical bar — interrupts the arrow. The arrow approaches from the left, meets the bar, and resumes on the right side. A label above the bar reads: "The Wall". This is the only element in the diagram that uses the accent colour.
- A thin horizontal arrow connects Stage 3 to Stage 4. Smooth, unbroken.

### Subtext strip

Below the four blocks, a single line of text spanning the full width: "Who builds changes. The CoE builds → Teams build on a platform → The platform improves itself."

## Style notes
- **Background:** off-white (`#FAFAFA`)
- **Stage blocks:** midnight (`#0F172A`) — 1px stroke, no fill, rounded corners (4px radius)
- **Dots, lines, platform bars, and arrows inside blocks:** midnight (`#0F172A`) — solid, 2px stroke, no fill
- **Feedback loop arrow in Stage 4:** midnight, same weight as other elements — no accent colour here
- **The Wall bar:** reserved violet (`#6D28D9`) — solid, 4px width — the only colour in the entire diagram, drawing the eye to the critical transition
- **"The Wall" label:** violet (`#6D28D9`), 13px, semibold — matching the bar
- **Connection arrows between stages:** midnight at 60% opacity, 1.5px stroke
- **Stage labels:** midnight, 14px, medium weight
- **Subtext strip:** midnight at 50% opacity, 12px, regular weight
- **Spacing:** equal gaps between all four stage blocks. The wall element sits centred in the gap between Stage 2 and Stage 3, visually wider than the other gaps to accommodate the bar and label.
- **No gradients, no shadows, no fills, no decoration.** Line art only.
