# Diagram Brief — Build · Buy · Boost

## Title
Three Strategies, One Gradient

## Single idea the diagram conveys
Build, Buy, and Boost place engineering effort in fundamentally different locations — and as the model commoditises, the weight of that effort shifts decisively toward the harness.

## Layout description
A vertical triptych — three stacked rows, one per strategy, read top to bottom: Build, Buy, Boost. Each row is a horizontal bar showing where engineering effort goes, divided into two segments: "Model" (left) and "Harness" (right).

1. **Build** — The bar is dominated by the Model segment (roughly 75%). The Harness segment is narrow. The label beneath reads "Build". A subtle annotation above the Model segment: "weights, training pipeline, retraining".

2. **Buy** — The bar is almost entirely absent — a thin sliver for "Integration" on the far right, with the rest greyed out (stippled or hatched at 15% opacity) to indicate the vendor owns both model and harness. The label beneath reads "Buy". A subtle annotation: "vendor owns the system".

3. **Boost** — The bar is dominated by the Harness segment (roughly 75%). The Model segment is narrow. The label beneath reads "Boost". A subtle annotation above the Harness segment: "data, evals, orchestration, steering".

A vertical arrow runs along the left edge of the three rows, pointing downward, labelled "model commoditises" — indicating the direction of the strategic shift.

The Boost row's Harness segment uses the reserved violet fill (`#6D28D9`) at 15% opacity with a violet left border — this is the only colour in the diagram, drawing the eye to where the dominant strategy concentrates its effort.

## Style notes
- **Background:** off-white (`#FAFAFA`)
- **Bar outlines and labels:** midnight (`#0F172A`) — solid, 2px stroke
- **Model segments:** midnight fill at 8% opacity (very light)
- **Harness segments (Build and Buy rows):** midnight fill at 8% opacity
- **Harness segment (Boost row only):** violet (`#6D28D9`) fill at 15% opacity, with a 3px left border in solid violet — the single accent element
- **Buy row greyed area:** midnight at 5% opacity with a diagonal hatch pattern
- **Typography:** strategy labels in midnight, 16px, semibold. Annotations in midnight at 50% opacity, 12px, regular weight. Vertical arrow label in midnight at 60% opacity, 12px, regular weight.
- **Spacing:** equal vertical gaps between the three rows. Rows are the same width. The composition reads as a single unit.
- **No gradients, no shadows, no rounded corners, no decoration.** Clean geometric bars only.
