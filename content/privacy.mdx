## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name and email address when you submit a contact form. We may also collect standard server logs including IP address, browser type, and pages visited.

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiries and improve the site experience. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained for a maximum of 12 months or until the matter is resolved, whichever is earlier. Server logs are retained for up to 90 days.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

## Contact
